# Recovery and attribution

**Author:** Itai Caspi. **Supervisor:** Aaron Wetzler. The report identifies Spring 2016; the official GIP catalog places the project under 2017. These are distinct dates.

The final-submission project page names `itaicaspi/keras-dqn-doom` as the agents implementation and links the score-parsing project separately. This confirms the association; it does not show that every file in the final report is included in the public repository.

## Original source

Source: https://github.com/itaicaspi/keras-dqn-doom

Pinned revision: `4241a994a49a39b88bfe5261b37d5abe6e0459a4` (16 October 2016).

`original/` contains the unchanged upstream README, `main.py`, `next_state_prediction.py`, four scenario configurations and eight referenced original figures. `original/SHA256.json` records their SHA-256 hashes. The remaining upstream files, including six checkpoints, stay available at the pinned link. The private submission archive and documents are retained privately for recovery; they are not uploaded here.

The upstream snapshot does not supply a software license. Public availability is not treated as a grant of unrestricted reuse, and no new license is imposed on Itai’s files or recordings. Author permission and any preferred credit/profile corrections are being requested. Doom/ViZDoom imagery retains the rights of its respective owners.

## Updated files

The `demo/` policy calculations and interface are new 2026 work by Aaron Wetzler with agent assistance. They are separate from `original/` and are not represented as Itai’s original implementation. The two browser MP4s are format conversions of the unchanged upstream OGV recordings:

- `assets/dqn-doom-1.ogv` → `demo/media/clip-1.mp4`
- `assets/dqn-doom-2.ogv` → `demo/media/clip-2.mp4`

No new training or neural-network inference was performed. The report’s result that learned macro-actions did not improve over standard DQN is retained; the archive does not turn the abstract’s research motivation into a claim of success.
